About QuickNotes

QuickNotes is a simple app for capturing short notes and ideas the moment they come to you. It keeps everything in one place so you can review your thoughts before they disappear.

How to use QuickNotes

  1. Open the QuickNotes home page.
  2. Type your note into the "New note" field.
  3. Press "Add Note" to add it to your list.
  4. Check "Your notes" to see everything you have saved.

Features

  • Add notes in seconds
  • See all your notes in one list
  • Live note counter
  • Clean, distraction-free layout

Keyboard shortcuts

Shortcut Action
Tab Move to the next field or button
Enter Add the note you have typed
Shift + Tab Move back to the previous field
